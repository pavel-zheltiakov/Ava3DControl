LightCollection

public sealed class LightCollection

The lights in a scene, up to LightCollection.Capacity of them.

The cap is what keeps the renderers a single forward pass: four lights is a fixed-size uniform block and one loop in the shader, with no structural difference from the one light this control started with. A fifth would mean either a second pass over every surface or a deferred pipeline, and neither is what a viewer control should grow into on its own.

Adding beyond the cap throws rather than dropping the light silently. A light that is simply not there is the kind of bug that gets diagnosed as a shader problem.
